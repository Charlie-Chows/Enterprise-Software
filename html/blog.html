TOPOLOGY · NOTE 041 · 9 MIN READ

Why your architecture diagram
is already out of date.

Diagrams decay because they are drawn once and read never. We treat the topology as a build artifact — generated, versioned, and diffed alongside the services it describes. When the model is generated from the same source as the deployment, drift stops being a surprise and becomes a warning.

  • KEY POINT Diagrams that are not generated are decorative
  • KEY POINT Drift should warn, not surprise
  • KEY POINT The model is the artifact, not the picture

EDITORIAL CODE

How the journal is written.

R-01

Named sources only

Every claim has an author. We do not publish anonymised notes attributed to "the team". If someone is going to stand behind a claim, they are named.

R-02

Placeholders are marked

Illustrative content is labelled as illustrative. We do not present sample data as if it were real. If a figure is invented for the sake of layout, it says so.

R-03

No silent edits

When a note is revised, the revision is visible. We do not quietly fix a wrong claim. The correction is part of the record.

R-04

Technical honesty over polish

If we do not know something, we say so. If a tradeoff exists, we describe it. A tidy story that is not true is worth less than a messy one that is.

R-05

Written for the operator

Every note is written for the person who will read it at 3am with no context. If a piece requires a phone call to understand, it is not finished.

R-06

No sponsored content

Everything published here is written by the people who work on the platform. Nothing is paid for, and nothing is placed.

Editorial code describes our practice. It is not a contractual commitment.
