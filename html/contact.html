FREQUENTLY ASKED

Answers to what
we are asked most.

If your question is not here, write it in. That is what the form is for.

Do you sign NDAs before the first call?

Yes, if that is what your process requires. We will also tell you honestly if a mutual NDA is unnecessary for the stage you are at — in most cases, the first call is a technical conversation, not a disclosure of anything sensitive.

Can we start with a small, bounded engagement?

Yes. The assessment stage exists for exactly that. It is bounded to the size of your estate and ends with a written artifact — you are free to stop there.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. Engagements are remote-first, with travel where it clearly helps. The studio in Salem is a working location, not a requirement for engagement.

What if we are not ready yet?

Write in anyway. It is common for teams to reach out a quarter before they intend to start, and it is useful for both sides to know each other before anything is committed.

Will you hand over to our internal team?

That is the intended shape of delivery. The runbook set is rehearsed with your operators, not ours, and the engagement is not considered complete until they can operate independently.

What is out of scope?

Application development, data migration, and managed off-hours support. The platform governs how systems talk to each other — it does not rewrite what those systems do.

BEFORE YOU WRITE

A short note
on how to write in.

Most enquiries that get a fast, useful reply share three things. None of them require preparation — they just make the first message easier to answer.

  1. 01

    Say what you actually operate.

    Not what you plan to operate, and not what the org chart says you own. What is running today, and who gets paged when it stops. That single sentence often reshapes the whole conversation.

  2. 02

    Name the constraint, if there is one.

    A regulatory boundary, a legacy system that cannot be moved, a team that cannot be retrained this year. Constraints are the most useful thing you can send — they define the shape of any real solution.

  3. 03

    Ask the question you actually have.

    If you are not sure whether we can help, ask that directly. It is a legitimate question, and we would rather answer it honestly than spend a call working out what you meant to ask.
